Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the following applications (collectively referred to as the "Applications"):

  • LumaLens: Eye Care
  • LumaLens: Eye Care Pro Unlocker

1. Zero-Telemetry Commitment

The Applications are built with a privacy-first, zero-telemetry philosophy. We believe that your device activity should remain private. The Applications do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data, usage statistics, or device identifiers.

2. Data Collection and Usage

We do not have access to any information that can identify you.

  • No Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, or phone numbers.
  • No Tracking: There are no analytics suites or tracking pixels integrated into the software.
  • No Cloud Storage: All application settings and configurations are stored locally on your device.

3. Permissions Explained

Permissions are requested based on the specific architectural needs of each application:

For LumaLens: Eye Care (Free):

  • System Alert Window (Appear on Top): Required to apply visual filters and eye-care masks locally across the screen interface.
  • Package Query: Used to check for the presence of the Pro Unlocker locally on your device to enable premium features.
  • Zero Network Access: This version does not request Internet or Billing permissions. It is a fully offline utility.

For LumaLens: Eye Care Pro Unlocker:

  • Internet & Google Play Billing: Required specifically to communicate with the Google Play Store for purchase verification, license validation, and the optional "Tip Jar" support.

4. Third-Party Services

Only the Pro Unlocker utilizes third-party services:

  • Google Play Billing: All financial transactions and optional tips are processed securely by Google. We do not receive or store your payment details. Please refer to Google’s Privacy Policy for more information.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

For any questions regarding this policy, please contact the developer via the official project repository on GitHub.
